## Given requirement
Build the Book table, load the provided sample data, and answer the ten assessment questions.

### Questions
- Q1: Books with price greater than 1500.
- Q2: Books published between 1900 and 2000, sorted by year.
- Q3: Fiction or Mystery books with stock greater than 5.
- Q4: Titles containing the word "the".
- Q5: Titles starting with A or ending with t.
- Q6: Books with no recorded author.
- Q7: Out-of-stock books or books with unknown publisher.
- Q8: Three most expensive books in stock.
- Q9: Urdu books sorted by published year.
- Q10: Books before 2000 with price under 1200, sorted by genre and title.
